# German

German UI text is a translation of the English meaning, not of its sentence. Write the sentence a German product would write, then check it says the same.

## Rules

- Address the reader with "Sie", always, with "Ihr" and imperatives in the Sie form ("Laden Sie Dateien hoch"). Buttons use the infinitive ("Speichern", "Repository löschen").
- Prefer verbs to nouns. "OrcAI bereitet die Datei vor" beats "Die Vorbereitung der Datei wird durchgeführt". Watch for "-ung" chains and "erfolgt", "durchgeführt", "vorgenommen".
- Punctuation follows the English rules. There is no Gedankenstrich as punctuation and no semicolon. Join closely linked halves with a conjunction ("und", "denn", "sodass", "aber") or restructure, and split only separate ideas into two sentences.
- Quotes in running text are „so“. UI labels quoted in text keep the German label.
- Never write "bitte". "Versuchen Sie es erneut." is polite enough.
- Error toasts follow the English shape, „Das Repository wurde nicht gelöscht.“ plus the reason.
- Use "außerdem", "zudem", "darüber hinaus" and "ferner" at most once per paragraph, and only when the sentence adds something.
- Sentence case applies to adjectives and verbs only. Nouns keep their capital as German grammar requires ("Neues Repository", not "Neues repository").
- Product terms and their plural forms come from the glossary's German entries (Repositories, Assets, Blöcke).
- Some glossary terms have no plural ("Verhalten"). Use the countable form the glossary gives (Verhaltensvorgaben) or rephrase ("Noch kein Verhalten").
- A compound takes a hyphen when a part is English or an abbreviation ("Chat-Verlauf", "Assistant-Modell", "Repository-Liste", "PDF-Datei"), otherwise it is one word ("Kontoeinstellungen"). "Chatverlauf" is a tell.

## Tells specific to German

- Nominalstil and abstract stacks ("die Umsetzung der Optimierung der Prozesse").
- Mechanical connectors opening sentence after sentence ("Darüber hinaus", "Zudem", "Ferner").
- Importance phrases ("Es ist wichtig zu beachten, dass", "spielt eine zentrale Rolle").
- "dient als", "fungiert als", "verfügt über" where "ist" or "hat" fits.
- Participle riders ("..., wodurch ein reibungsloser Ablauf gewährleistet wird").
- Anglicisms in grammar, not vocabulary ("Sinn machen", "am Ende des Tages").
- English Title Case in headings ("Die Neue Bibliothek").
- Register drift, du and Sie in one text, or a chatty opener ("Machen wir einen Rundgang").
