OrcAI is a self-hostable platform for building governed AI bots around curated knowledge. It supports individual, team, education and research work. Members upload files, OrcAI prepares and indexes them, and users chat with bots that retrieve relevant context from those assets.

OrcAI is in beta and changes often.

## Main capabilities

- AI-assisted chat grounded in curated source content
- The OrcAI Assistant, a built-in helper that answers questions about your work and about OrcAI itself
- Asset preparation for PDFs, Office files, and images
- Background preparation and indexing workflows
- Qdrant-backed vector search for retrieval-augmented generation
- S3-compatible storage for uploaded and prepared assets
- Organisation, group, invitation, and access management
- Configurable providers, models, quota pools, observability, and email delivery
