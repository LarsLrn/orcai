OrcAI lets organisations build bots that answer from their own material and cite their sources. It originated in the [SokratesT project](https://github.com/SokratesT/sokratest) and still supports learning workflows where instructors curate sources and students interact with a grounded AI tutor. Beyond that fixed instructor-student model, individuals and groups can build specialised repositories and bots for their own domains.

## What OrcAI does

1. Members upload files to the Library, and OrcAI prepares them as assets.
2. Repositories group assets, and behaviours set how a bot writes.
3. A bot combines one behaviour with its repositories and features such as web search or questions and quizzes.
4. When someone asks a bot a question, it searches its repositories for matching passages and cites them in its answer.

A chat can also run on a model alone, without a bot.

Behind this, background workers read the files, an embedding model indexes them in Qdrant, and the organisation's chat models write the answers. See [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting) for the services an instance runs.

## Core areas

- Home (`/app`): the start page after sign-in, with a Use tab (`/app?view=use`) for asking questions and a Manage tab (`/app/manage`) with an overview of the organisation and, for admins, its readiness checklist.
- Chat (`/app/chat`): AI chats, optionally started from a bot.
- Library (`/app/library`): everything the organisation can use, browsed by kind (Bots, Behaviours, Repositories and Assets) from one rail, with search, filters and a Mine switch. It is one item in the sidebar.
- Catalogue (`/app/catalogue`): the bots, behaviours and repositories that organisations on this instance have listed, to preview and add to your organisation. It is the sidebar item after Library.
- OrcAI Assistant (`/app/assistant`): the built-in helper for questions about your work and about OrcAI.
- Access control: organisations, roles, groups, invitations and sharing.
- Administration: Members, Groups, Quota pools, Analytics, Providers, Models and Organisation for the active organisation, shown to members whose role allows them.
- Instance management (`/instance`): health, organisations and accounts across the whole instance, for instance admins.

## Getting access

The managed OrcAI service is open only by invitation from someone already on the instance. To create your own organisation and invite people yourself, contact us at [sokratest@hochschule-rhein-waal.de](mailto:sokratest@hochschule-rhein-waal.de).
