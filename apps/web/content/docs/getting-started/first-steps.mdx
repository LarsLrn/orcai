Join an organisation by invitation, or initialise a new instance and prepare its first organisation.

## Join an organisation

1. Open the link in your invitation email, and **Create your account** opens with your email address filled in. Enter your name and a password, agree to the privacy policy and terms of use, and choose **Create account**. If you already have an account, sign in and accept the invitation on the organisation selection page instead. See [Invitations](/docs/using-orcai/account#invitations).
2. If OrcAI asks you to select an organisation, choose the one you joined. See [Active organisation](/docs/using-orcai/access-control#active-organisation).
3. Open the **Use** tab on Home. The first time, a short [tour](/docs/using-orcai#tour) shows the sidebar, **Start a chat**, your chats and your account.
4. Ask a question in the composer on the Use tab, or choose **Start a chat** in the sidebar to pick a bot or a model first. See [Chat](/docs/using-orcai/chat).
5. Ask the [OrcAI Assistant](/docs/using-orcai/assistant) when you want to know how something in OrcAI works.

To protect your account, add [two-factor authentication](/docs/using-orcai/account#two-factor-authentication) or a [passkey](/docs/using-orcai/account#passkeys) on the Account page.

## Set up a new instance

1. Run OrcAI and its services, for example with [Docker Compose](/docs/self-hosting/quickstart-compose).
2. Open the app, and a new instance leads to **Initialise instance**, where you create the first organisation and your account as its Admin and as instance admin. See [Initialise an instance](/docs/using-orcai/administration#initialise-an-instance).
3. Work through the **Readiness checklist** on Home's Manage tab. A chat needs an enabled provider, a chat model and a quota pool that covers the person and the model. The OrcAI Assistant also needs the organisation's Assistant model and a quota pool that covers that model. [Organisation setup](/docs/using-orcai/organisation-setup) walks through these steps and then through uploading assets, building a bot and inviting people.
