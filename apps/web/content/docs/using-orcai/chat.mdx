Chat is the core end-user workflow in OrcAI. You can start chats with or without a bot, attach sources, branch a conversation, and tune per-chat settings.

Conversations with the [OrcAI Assistant](/docs/using-orcai/assistant) live on their own page; the chat list shows them only in search results.

## Entry Points

- **Start a chat** in the sidebar, or on the chat list (`/app/chat`), opens the chat setup page (`/app/chat/setup`).
- The **Ask a question** composer on the Use tab of Home (`/app`) starts a chat directly, with bot and model pickers.
- **Start chat** on a bot's page (`/app/hub/bots/<bot id>`) starts a chat with that bot.

The setup page (`/app/chat/setup`) offers two options:

- **Use a bot**: opens the **Select a bot** dialog, which lists published bots you can access. **Start chat** continues with the chosen bot.
- **Chat without a bot**: answers come from the model alone.

Both go to the new chat page (`/app/chat/new`, with `?botId=<bot id>` when a bot was chosen), where you send the first message.

## Starting A New Chat

### Choose A Starting Point [step]

Use a bot when you want a predefined behaviour and set of repositories. Chat without a bot when you want answers from the model alone. On the new chat page (`/app/chat/new`) the composer still lets you change the bot, including **No bot**.

### Configure The First Message [step]

Before sending the first message, choose:

- Bot (optional)
- Model (required). OrcAI preselects the first chat model it finds; change it with the model picker in the composer.

The first message is text only. Attachments, behaviour and repositories are added once the chat exists.

The composer is disabled with a notice when no chat model is available (a model needs Text and Tool calling) or when no quota pool covers the chosen model for you. Members who can fix it get a link: **Set up a model** to Models (`/app/models`) or **Create a quota pool** to `/app/quotas/add`. Everyone else is asked to contact an organisation admin.

### Send And Continue [step]

When you send the first message, OrcAI creates the chat and opens it at `/app/chat/<chat id>`.

## Chat List

The chat list (`/app/chat`) shows all your chats in a table and supports:

- Search across titles and message text
- Filter by bot
- Sorting and pagination
- Pin, rename and delete per chat, from the row menu
- **Delete selected** as the only bulk action, available for any chats you select

The sidebar's **Chats** group shows your most recent chats, up to 40 together with the pinned ones, with the same pin, rename and delete actions.

### Pinning

**Pin chat** in a chat's menu keeps it at the top: in the sidebar's **Pinned** group, above **Chats**, and first in the chat list and on Home, marked with a pin, the most recently pinned first. Sorting the chat list by a column or searching orders pinned chats like any other. **Unpin chat** returns it to its place by last activity. Pins are your own, and a branch of a pinned chat starts unpinned. Assistant conversations cannot be pinned.

### Search

The search box on the chat list finds your own chats by title and by the text of their messages, questions and answers alike. It also finds your OrcAI Assistant conversations of the active organisation. Tool steps, attachments and source passages are not searched.

- Every word must appear; the last one also matches as the start of a word, so results follow as you type. Case does not matter, and words match exactly in any language, without stemming.
- Results are ordered by relevance, with title matches first, unless you sort by a column.
- A result found in a message shows a short excerpt with the matching words marked. Opening it shows the chat at that message, which is highlighted briefly; older messages still load as you scroll up.

## In-Conversation Features

In a chat (`/app/chat/<chat id>`), OrcAI supports:

- Streaming model responses, with Stop while an answer is generated
- Message actions: **Copy** and **Branch from here** on your questions; **Copy**, **Branch from here** and **Rate response** on answers
- Ratings stay private to you; organisation Admins see only how many answers of each bot were rated how, on [Analytics](/docs/using-orcai/administration#analytics)
- Usage metadata on assistant messages: the tokens a reply used, and how full the model's context window was when the model's context window is known; a reply that summarized the chat counts the summary too
- Switching the model with the model picker in the composer
- The composer's **More chat actions** menu, with **Start tour**, **Chat settings** and **Download chat** (always the whole chat)
- Older messages load as you scroll up; a chat opens on its newest 20

### Long Chats

When a chat fills 80% of the model's context window, after room for the reply, its instructions and its tools, the earlier conversation is summarized for the model. While the summary is written, the reply shows **Compacting conversation…**; afterwards a divider **Earlier messages summarized** marks where the model's view starts from the summary. The summary itself is not shown, and the chat keeps every message. OpenAI models, and Anthropic models whose budget allows a trigger of at least 50,000 tokens, summarize on the provider's side, also in the middle of a long answer. Other models write the summary as a separate call before the answer, which counts against your quota like the reply.

If a summary cannot be written, the model reads only the newest turns that fill about three quarters of the window, and the reply says so under it. A reply whose next step would not fit stops with a notice; send a message to continue. If your message alone does not fit, it is not sent: shorten it, remove attachments, or start a new chat.

A model without a known context window is assumed to read 128,000 tokens. Admins can set a smaller **Context window** on the model to summarize earlier.

## Branching

Every message has a **Branch from here** action. It creates a new chat that copies the history, the bot, the settings and the attached blocks, and opens it. The original chat stays as it was.

- From an answer: the branch holds the conversation up to and including that answer, with an empty composer.
- From a question: the branch holds the conversation before that question, and the composer is filled with its text and attachments so you can change it before sending. Nothing is sent automatically.

Branches are ordinary chats. They keep the original title, show a branch icon in the sidebar and the chat list, and link back with **Branched from …** above the conversation. In the original chat, the message a branch started from lists its **Branches**.

## Attachments In Chat

Attachments are added to messages in an existing chat, not to the first message. Use **Add file** in the composer:

- **Upload file**: upload a file from your device. When you send the message, the file becomes an asset of yours, listed under **Chat uploads** on the Assets page, and OrcAI prepares it like any other asset.
- **From assets**: pick assets from the Library or your chat uploads (**Select existing assets**); chat uploads carry a **Chat upload** badge. The asset is attached to that one message only, not to the chat.

Limits:

- Up to 8 attachments per message
- Up to 25 MB per uploaded file
- Accepted types: JPEG, PNG, GIF and WebP images, PDF, TXT, CSV and Markdown, DOCX and PPTX
- Text extracted from an attached document is cut off after 20,000 characters
- Up to 20 MB of images go to the model per turn, the most recent first; older images beyond that are left out with a note

You can send text only, attachments only, or text with attachments.

You can attach only assets you can read. The model reads a message's attachments again on every later turn while you can still read them; once you lose access to an asset or it is deleted, the model is told that attachment is no longer available.

## Chat Settings

Open **Chat settings** from the composer's **More chat actions** menu. You can configure:

- Model
- System prompt (only for chats not linked to a bot)
- **Advanced generation settings**
	- Temperature
	- Top P
	- Max tokens
	- Frequency penalty
	- Presence penalty

A generation setting you leave empty is not sent, so the provider's own default applies. Clear a setting to return it to the provider default. Max tokens is the exception: it limits what the model writes in each step of a reply, and when it is empty a step writes at most 16,384 tokens, or the model's max output tokens or half its context window when that is smaller.

Max tokens cannot exceed the model's max output tokens when the model has them set. If the model changes or its limit is lowered later, a reply asks for the model's max output tokens instead.

For bot-linked chats:

- The system prompt is locked to the bot's template block
- Attached blocks are managed by the bot, not in chat settings
- Features are managed by the bot; the settings show them read-only

For chats without a bot, **Chat blocks** holds:

- **Add behaviour**: attach one behaviour block. Remove it to attach another.
- **Add repository**: attach one or more repositories to retrieve from.

In such a chat you can also turn features such as Generative UI on and off; each switch saves immediately. Generative UI is on by default for new chats. Web search and Questions & quizzes are bot features and are not offered here.

## Generative UI

With the Generative UI feature enabled, the model can place structured elements, called primitives, in its answer. A primitive appears where the model places it, between the paragraphs of the answer, and fills in while the answer streams. Primitives only show content; they never ask for input.

### Checklist

A checklist is a card with one row per item. Rows can expand to show an explanation and a quoted passage; several rows can be open at once.

- With statuses, each row shows Met, Partly met, Not met or Not checkable, and the header counts the outcomes. This suits checking something against a list of criteria.
- Without statuses, the checklist is a plain accordion of expandable points.

### Review

A review shows one or more texts checked against rules, such as a reference list against a style guide. Each text gets a status from its most serious finding, and the header counts them.

- The Findings view shows each text with the passages a finding refers to underlined and numbered, followed by the findings with matching numbers. Findings about all texts together appear last, under Overall.
- When the model suggests corrected versions, Changes shows each text with removed and added words marked, or before and after when most of it was rewritten, and Revised shows the corrected texts.
- Copying a revision, or all of them with Copy all, keeps italics when you paste into a word processor; plain-text targets get the text without formatting marks.

### Matrix

A matrix is a table of several items checked against the same criteria, one row per item and one column per criterion, with a status icon in each cell. The header counts the statuses. A row with notes on cells that are not met ends in a notes button that expands the notes; several rows can be open at once.

### Turning The Feature Off

Primitives already stored in a chat keep rendering after Generative UI is turned off. The model just stops placing new ones.

### Copy And Download

Copying an answer and downloading the conversation include primitives as markdown, in the order they appear in the answer. A checklist becomes a list with each row's status, summary, quote and explanation; a review becomes each text as a quote with its findings and revision; a matrix becomes a table of statuses followed by its notes.

## Quota Line

When quota data is available, a line above the composer shows the pool that covers the chat's model and what is left in it, either requests or tokens.

## Preparing Documents

When a repository the chat searches still has documents that are not ready, a **Preparing documents** panel sits above the composer, under the quota line. The repositories are the bot's and those attached to the chat; on the new chat page they are the chosen bot's. The panel shows how many of all documents are ready, for example `38/120`, with a spinner while documents are being prepared. Once only failures are left, its title says how many documents could not be prepared. It disappears when every document is ready and refreshes every five seconds while documents are being prepared.

The panel is collapsed. Expand it for one row per repository with its status, how many of its documents are ready and how many failed. A repository you can edit links to its page, where you can retry failed documents. Only ready documents are searchable, so the model can tell you when the answer may be in a document that is not ready yet. The panel never blocks sending.

## Retrieval And Citations

When a chat has repositories, through its bot or its chat settings, the model can search them before it answers, but it may also answer directly. An answer that took steps shows one work log above it, listing the steps, such as **Searched the sources**, **Read the passages** and **Read a page**, and any interim text the model wrote between them; the text after the last step is the answer. While a step runs, a line under the answer names it. A search returns whole passages, and reading the passages fetches the text around one of them. Expand a step to see the passages it found, each shortened to three lines with **Show more** to read the rest.

A reply takes at most 25 steps, 20 in the OrcAI Assistant, and fewer when its quota pool counts requests and has fewer left. On the last one the model is asked to answer from what it has found and to say what it could not finish. A reply that still ends without an answer says it stopped at the step limit.

A reply runs for at most 30 minutes. One that reaches the limit stops as if you had stopped it: what it wrote is kept, it is charged for the steps it took, and a notice under it says it reached its time limit. Send a message to continue.

Answers cite their sources inline. A citation shows the asset's title and page, and opening it shows the source details with an **Open asset** link to the asset's page (`/app/hub/assets/<asset id>`). Copying an answer writes each citation as the cited text followed by its source's title and page in parentheses.

## Web Search

In a chat with a bot that has the Web search feature on, the model can search the web while the organisation's [web search setting](/docs/using-orcai/administration#web-search) allows it. Each search is a step in the work log, such as **Searched the web for “orca pods”, 3 pages**; expand it to see the pages it found, each linking to the page. Pages the answer cites are listed under it as **Sources**, each linking to the page. With the allowed domains set, only pages on those domains and their subdomains are searched.

When the organisation's web search is off, or the setting is model native and the chat's model has no search of its own, a notice above the composer says so and the bot answers without searching.

## Questions And Quizzes

In a chat with a bot that has the Questions & quizzes feature on, the model can ask you questions in the conversation instead of in its text, and run quizzes it grades. The reply waits for your answers and continues once you send them. Questions can offer one choice, several choices, a free-text answer or a scale between two values.

### Questions

When the model needs your input, it asks up to five questions, one per step. Choosing one of several options moves to the next question; for several choices, a free-text answer or a scale, select **Next**. Choice questions also offer an **Other** field for an answer of your own. Every question is optional. After the last question, a review lists your answers; select one to go back to it. **Submit** sends what you have answered so far, on any step and even with nothing answered. **Skip** tells the model you did not want to answer. Writing a new message instead skips the questions too, and the model reads your message as your reply.

### Quizzes

A quiz has up to 20 questions in titled sections, one section per page, with **Next** and **Back** between pages and **Submit answers** on the last. Choice questions offer only their options. Every question counts, and one left blank is wrong; the last page says how many are unanswered. When the questions are worth different points, each shows its points. The correct answers are not sent to your browser before you submit.

After you submit, the model grades every question as correct, partly correct or incorrect and shows the result in its answer: the score, each section's subtotal, and one line per question. A question you did not get fully right also shows your answer, the expected answer, or the key points of a free-text answer, and why. OrcAI adds up the points, so the totals always match the questions. **Practise what I got wrong** asks the model for practice on what you missed.

Answers are final. Afterwards the questions stay in the conversation, read-only, with the answers you gave. Copying an answer and downloading the conversation include the questions with your answers and the graded quiz as markdown.

## Deleting Chats

Deleting a chat removes it and its messages for good. Chats branched from it stay, without the **Branched from** link.
