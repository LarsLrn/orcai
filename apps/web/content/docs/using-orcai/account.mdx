The account page is your personal control panel inside the active organisation context.

## Available Sections

- User stats summary card
- Email verification status and resend action
- Change password form
- Profile form
- Organisation invitations list, with Pending, Accepted, and Rejected tabs and
  Previous/Next pagination within each tab
- Legal links (privacy policy and terms of use)

## Email Verification

The account screen displays whether your email is verified.

If not verified, you can trigger Send Verification Email directly from the page.

## Password

Password changes are handled inline through the Change Password form.

For forgotten passwords, the app also has a dedicated forgot/reset flow outside the authenticated area.

## Invitations

Registration is by invitation. An account is created from an invitation link
sent by an administrator of the organisation you are joining; there is no open
sign-up. The register page names the inviting organisation, and the address is
fixed by the invitation.

Signing up requires the invitation ID from the link and a matching address.
It accepts only that invitation. Other pending invitations stay visible in the
organisation picker, even with one active organisation. With one active
organisation and no pending invitations you enter it directly. Email
verification remains optional for access.

Invitations that arrive after your account exists are listed on the account
page and in the organisation selection flow, where you accept or reject them.

Switching organisation takes you to the organisation selection page.
