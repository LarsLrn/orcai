The Account page holds your personal settings. Open it from **Account** in the user menu at the bottom of the sidebar.

## Tabs

- **Profile** (`/app/account`): a summary card with your Name and Email, **Email verification** and **Your profile**
- **Security** (`/app/account/security`): **Change password** and **Two-factor authentication**
- **Invitations** (`/app/account/invitations`): **Organisation invitations**, with Pending, Accepted and Rejected tabs
- **Data** (`/app/account/data`): **Export your data**, and **Legal** with links to the privacy policy and terms of use

## Summary Card

The card at the top shows your Name and Email as stored on your account. It shows no usage figures. Quota usage appears in each chat above the composer.

## Email Verification

The **Email verification** card says whether your email address is verified.

While it is not verified, the card offers **Send verification email**, which sends a new verification link. Once verified, the button no longer shows. Verification is optional for access.

## Profile

The **Your profile** card edits your Name. Save it with **Save profile**. There is no avatar upload, and your email address cannot be changed here.

## Password

The **Change password** card asks for your Current password, a New password and Confirm new password. Submitting with **Change password** asks you to confirm before the password changes.

If you have forgotten your password, use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. It opens `/forgot-password`, where you request a reset email. The link in that email opens `/reset-password`, where you choose a new password.

## Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) asks for a code from an authenticator app, such as a password manager or a phone app, after your password. Set it up on the Security tab:

1. Select **Set up two-factor authentication** and confirm your password.
2. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, or enter the **Setup key** by hand.
3. Enter the six-digit code the app shows and select **Verify and turn on**.
4. Keep the ten backup codes shown next somewhere safe: copy or download them. They are shown only once, and each signs you in once when you cannot use your authenticator app.

When you sign in, OrcAI then asks for a code from the app. **Use a backup code** switches to a backup code. **Trust this device for 30 days** skips the code on this browser for 30 days.

On the Security tab, **New backup codes** replaces your backup codes, and **Turn off** turns 2FA off; both ask for your password. Turning 2FA off sends you an email. When the administrator of your instance requires 2FA for you, **Turn off** is missing, and signing in without it set up takes you to a setup page first.

If you lose your authenticator app, sign in with a backup code and set up 2FA again. Without a backup code, ask the instance administrator to reset your 2FA.

## Invitations

Registration is by invitation. An account is created from an invitation link
sent by an administrator of the organisation you are joining; there is no open
sign-up. The link opens the register page (`/register`), which names the
inviting organisation, and the address is fixed by the invitation.

Signing up requires the invitation ID from the link and a matching address.
It accepts only that invitation. Other pending invitations stay visible in the
organisation picker, even with one active organisation. With one active
organisation and no pending invitations you enter it directly. Email
verification remains optional for access.

Invitations that arrive after your account exists are listed in the
**Organisation invitations** card on the Invitations tab and on the organisation
selection page (`/select-organization`), where you **Accept** or **Reject**
them. The card shows Pending first, with 12 invitations per page and Previous
and Next buttons within each tab.

## Data Export

The **Export your data** card builds a zip archive of your data. Start it with **Export my data**; the card shows that the export is being prepared, and you can leave the page meanwhile. Only one export runs at a time.

The archive holds:

- `profile.json`: your name, email, language, preferences, and organisation memberships with their roles
- `ratings.json` and `favourites.json`: your message ratings and favourite bots
- `bots.json`, `blocks.json` and `assets.json`: the bots, blocks and assets you own
- `chats/`: each of your chats, including OrcAI Assistant conversations, as JSON with all messages and as a Markdown transcript like the one **Download chat** saves
- `files/`: the original file of each asset you uploaded

When the archive is ready, the card offers **Download** and you receive an email that links to the Data tab. The download stays available for 24 hours, then the archive is deleted. You can start a new export once the previous one has finished or failed.

## Organisation Context And Switching

The user menu at the bottom of the sidebar shows your active organisation and includes **Switch organisation**.

**Switch organisation** opens **Select your organisation** (`/select-organization`), where you pick another organisation you belong to and see pending invitations. Instance administrators also find a **Manage instance** link there.

## Legal

The **Legal** section links to the Privacy policy (`/privacy`) and the Terms of use (`/tou`). Both open in a new browser tab.
