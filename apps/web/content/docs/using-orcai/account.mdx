title: Account

The account page is your personal control panel inside the active organisation context.

## Available Sections

- User stats summary card
- Email verification status and resend action
- Change password form
- Profile form
- Organisation invitations list
- Legal links (privacy policy and terms of use)

## Email Verification

The account screen displays whether your email is verified.

If not verified, you can trigger Send Verification Email directly from the page.

## Profile

Profile data is editable from the Your Profile card.

## Password

Password changes are handled inline through the Change Password form.

For forgotten passwords, the app also has a dedicated forgot/reset flow outside the authenticated area.

## Invitations

Organisation invitations are visible in account and can also appear in the organisation selection flow.

The account page includes a user stats card loaded asynchronously.

## Organisation Context And Switching

The user menu (bottom sidebar profile menu) shows your active organisation and includes a Switch Organisation action.

Switching organisation takes you to the organisation selection page.

The account page links to:

- Privacy policy
- Terms of use
