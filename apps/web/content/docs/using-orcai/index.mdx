This section documents the actual UX flows currently implemented in the main OrcAI app.

## Who This Is For

- People who chat with OrcAI using bots or direct model selection.
- People who curate reusable content and bot configurations.
- Workspace admins who manage users, groups, models, providers, and quotas.

## App Structure At A Glance

The app requires authentication and an active organisation. Everything below belongs to the active organisation; switch organisation to see another one.

### Home

Home has two tabs, each with its own address:

- **Use** (`/app`): a greeting with **Take a tour**, the chat composer with bot and model pickers and attachments (sending creates a chat), and a card for your active organisation with how many bots, behaviours and repositories it lists in the catalogue, a link to its **Organisation page** and, for admins, **Edit**. Below the composer, **Try something** offers up to three bots to start a chat with, favourites first, and the newest catalogue entry your organisation has not added yet. **Recent chats** lists the four chats you used most recently.
- **Manage** (`/app/manage`): the organisation's name with **Create a bot**, for admins the **Organisation setup** checklist, and the **Library**, **Recently changed** and **People** panels. People who manage quotas also get the **Quota** panel with each pool's amounts; everyone else sees their pool's share in the chat composer.

While a required setup step is missing, admins who open `/app` land on Manage, and the Manage tab shows how many required steps are left. See [Workspace Setup For Admins](/docs/using-orcai/workspace-setup).

### Sidebar

- **Start a chat** opens the chat setup (`/app/chat/setup`).
- The **Chats** group lists your 40 most recent chats, with rename and delete in each chat's menu. **Show all** opens the chat list (`/app/chat`).
- The **Your workspace** group holds:
	- **OrcAI Assistant** (`/app/assistant`), available to every member.
	- **Library** (`/app/library`), always shown. Its items **Bots** (`/app/library/bots`), **Behaviour** (`/app/library/behaviours`), **Repositories** (`/app/library/repositories`) and **Assets** (`/app/library/assets`) need the `read` permission.
	- **Administration**, shown when your role allows at least one of its items: **Users** (`/app/users`), **Groups** (`/app/groups`), **Quotas** (`/app/quotas`), **Providers** (`/app/providers`), **Models** (`/app/models`) and **Organisation** (`/app/organization`). See [Access control](/docs/using-orcai/access-control) for which roles see which items.
- The user menu at the bottom holds **Account** (`/app/account`), **Switch organisation** (`/select-organization`), **Manage instance** (`/instance`, for instance administrators only) and **Sign out**.

### Header

The header shows a breadcrumb trail starting from Home, a **Language** switcher and a **Toggle theme** switch.

## Important Notes

- The app supports draft and published states for bots and blocks.
- Resource-level sharing exists for assets, blocks, and bots via access dialogs.
- Some UI terms currently use British spelling (for example, Organisation, Behaviour).
