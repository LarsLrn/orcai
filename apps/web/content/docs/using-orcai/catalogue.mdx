The catalogue (`/app/catalogue`) shows the bots, behaviours and repositories
that organisations on this instance list. Everyone signed in can browse it and
open an entry's page. Using an entry needs your organisation to add it and to
share it with you. Listing and what it shares are described under
[Access Control](/docs/using-orcai/access-control#the-catalogue).

## Browsing

The catalogue has the Library's layout. Its header has a search field; a rail
on the left (above the results on small screens) has:

- The views: **Discover**, and one per kind (Bots, Behaviours, Repositories)
  with its count.
- The view's filters, on a kind view or while searching. On narrow screens
  they open from **Filters**, which shows how many are set.

| View | Filters |
|---|---|
| Bots | **Model** (Any, Works with any model, Tuned for a model, Requires a model), **Organisation**, **Hide added** |
| Behaviours, Repositories | **Organisation**, **Hide added** |
| Discover, while searching | **Organisation**, **Hide added** |

**Discover** shows the newest entries, then a shelf per kind with **See all**.
While there are fewer than six entries it shows them in one grid. A search from
Discover covers every kind; on a kind view it covers that kind. Results sort
by **Newest** or **A to Z** and page like the Library. Search, filters, sort
and page are part of the address, so going back from an entry's page returns
to the same results at the same scroll position.

Each tile names the organisation the entry comes from and what it holds: a
bot's behaviour and repositories, "Prompt" for a behaviour, or a repository's
documents. A bot's tile shows the model its author set up: **Requires …** when
its chats always use that model, **Tuned for …** when it is a recommendation.
A check on the icon marks what your organisation already added.

## An Entry's Page

Opening a tile shows the entry's page: its organisation and owner, its
description, and what you need to judge it before adding it.

- A bot lists its behaviour and repositories, with their documents, and says
  which model it asks for and whether your organisation can use that model.
- A behaviour shows its full prompt.
- A repository shows its first documents and how many more it holds.

Admins and managers see **Add to …** for your active organisation. Other
members are told to ask them. An entry your organisation added shows
**Added to …** and **Open in Library**; one it made itself shows **Open in
Library**.

## Adding An Entry

**Add to …** adds the entry to your organisation and opens its setup:

1. **Sharing**: who in your organisation may use it. Nobody can until you
   share it. Grants here are always for use: your organisation cannot edit,
   list or delete what it added.
2. **Model** (bots only): the model the bot's chats use in your organisation,
   recommended or required, with the same settings and coverage as the bot
   editor's Model step. When the author set up a model that your organisation
   also has and may use, it is preselected as the **Author's pick**; otherwise
   pick another model or ask an admin to enable it. **Skip** leaves people
   free to pick any model.
3. **Done**: start a chat with the bot (once it is shared with you), open it
   in the Library, or go back to the catalogue.

Leaving the setup early keeps the entry added but unshared; finish it later
from the entry's **Access & groups** and, for a bot, its **Model in …** card in
the Library. The entry then appears in your Library marked "From" its home
organisation, and the **Source** filter there separates it from what your
organisation made. **Remove from …** in its **…** menu takes it out again.
Changes its home organisation makes reach you right away, and taking it out
of the catalogue removes it from every organisation that added it.

The [OrcAI Assistant](/docs/using-orcai/assistant) can search the catalogue
and, for admins and managers, add and remove entries after you confirm.
